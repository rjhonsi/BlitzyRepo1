The cardboard boxes were fortresses, and Lena was their sole defender. Outside, the city roared with a rhythm she didn't understand. The coffee shop barista knew her order before she said it, a small victory. A stray cat, a ginger with a torn ear, began waiting for her by the apartment steps each evening, a silent, furry anchor in the sea of strangers.
One day, she found a hand-drawn map in her mailbox, marking the nearest park, the best bakery, and a hidden library garden. It was unsigned. She looked out the window, and for the first time, the city didn't feel like a maze, but a place that was slowly, quietly, beginning to know her too.
